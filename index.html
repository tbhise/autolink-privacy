Privacy Policy — Autolink

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Autolink ("the app") is a personal tool, run by Tushar Bhise (tusharbhise908@gmail.com), that drafts LinkedIn posts and, only after the account owner approves each one, publishes them to the owner's own LinkedIn profile through LinkedIn's official API.

What we collect

  • From LinkedIn, when you connect your account: your LinkedIn member ID, your name, and an access token that lets the app publish posts you approve. We do not receive or store your LinkedIn password.
  • From you: your email address, a hashed password, the profile facts you enter, and the drafts you create or approve.
  • About published posts: impressions, reactions, comments, reposts and clicks, when LinkedIn provides them or when you enter them yourself.
  • Security records: IP address and browser type for sign-ins and security events.

How we use it

Only to research topics, write drafts, publish the posts you approve, show your post statistics, and keep your account secure. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.

Who we share it with

  • LinkedIn, to publish the posts you approve.
  • OpenAI, which receives the text needed to write and check drafts (topic, research and your profile facts). LinkedIn tokens are never sent to OpenAI.

No one else.

Where it is stored

On a private server we control. Connections use HTTPS. LinkedIn access tokens and two-factor secrets are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM).

How long we keep it

Until you delete it or ask us to. Sign-in sessions expire within 30 days. Disconnecting LinkedIn in the app stops all use of the token immediately; you can also revoke access in LinkedIn under Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Other applications → Permitted services.

Cookies

Only the cookies needed to keep you signed in, protect forms (CSRF) and, if you choose "Remember me", fill in the login form. No tracking or advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, its correction, or its deletion by emailing tusharbhise908@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when this policy changes.
